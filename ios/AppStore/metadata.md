## Promotional text
Write once, schedule everywhere — manage every connected social channel from one calendar.

## Description
Cuanto Socials is the mobile companion for your Cuanto Socials workspace. Draft a post once,
tailor it per channel, and schedule it on a calendar you can actually read on a phone.

PLAN ON A CALENDAR
See everything queued in day, week, month, or list view. Drag-free rescheduling: open a post,
pick a new time, done. Drafts are saved on the device, so a half-written post survives a lost
signal and syncs when you are back online.

POST TO EVERY CHANNEL
Compose once and override per channel where it matters — text, media, and channel-specific
limits are checked before the post is queued, not after it fails. Attach photos and video from
your library; uploads resume in the background.

WRITE WITH AI HELP
An optional AI copilot suggests copy, generates images, and drafts video concepts. It is off
until you turn it on, and every AI feature asks for consent before the first use.

STAY IN THE LOOP
Push notifications when a post publishes, when one fails, and when a channel needs to be
reconnected — each one is a separate toggle. Analytics show reach and engagement per channel
without leaving the app.

MANAGE THE WORKSPACE
Switch between organizations, connect or reconnect channels, review your signed-in devices,
and revoke any of them. Delete your account and all of its data from inside the app.

Cuanto Socials requires an existing Cuanto Socials account. Sign in with Apple, Google, or
email.

## App Review notes
Cuanto Socials is the client for a self-hosted Cuanto Socials (social scheduling) workspace and
requires an account on that workspace to do anything past the sign-in screen. A demo account is
provided in the App Review demo-account fields; it is pre-loaded with connected sample channels
and scheduled posts so every tab has content.

Notes for the reviewer:
- Sign in with the provided email and password on the first screen (Apple and Google sign-in
  also work, but the demo account is email/password).
- The calendar tab opens on the current week and already contains scheduled posts.
- The AI copilot, image, and video features ask for consent on first use; declining leaves the
  rest of the app fully usable.
- Push notifications are optional; the app never blocks on the permission prompt.
- No account creation, purchase, or payment happens inside the app. The app is free and
  contains no in-app purchases or ads.

## App Privacy (questionnaire answers)
No public ASC API for the nutrition label — fill these answers in the ASC web UI once.

Data collected and linked to the user:
- Contact info (email address) — App Functionality (account sign-in).
- User content (posts, photos, videos the user uploads to publish) — App Functionality.
- Identifiers (account ID, device ID for session and push routing) — App Functionality.

Data collected and not linked to the user:
- Diagnostics (crash and error counts, self-hosted) — App Functionality. Analytics are opt-out
  in Settings.

Not collected: precise or coarse location, contacts, health, financial info, browsing history,
search history, purchases, sensitive info.

Tracking: none. No data is used to track users across apps or websites, no advertising
identifier, no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
